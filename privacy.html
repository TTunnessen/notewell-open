Notewell privacy policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

What Notewell is

Notewell is a personal notes and flashcards app. Your notes, decks and cards are stored on your own devices.

Google account data

If you choose to turn on sync, Notewell asks Google for two things:

  • Your email address, only to show which account is signed in.
  • Access to Google Drive's app-data folder (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata). This is a hidden folder that only Notewell can see. Notewell stores one sync file there containing your notes, decks and cards, so your devices stay the same. Notewell can't see, read or change any other files in your Google Drive.

Sign-in tokens are kept only on your device. Your data goes directly between your device and Google. There is no Notewell server, and nothing is sent to anyone else.

What Notewell doesn't do

  • It doesn't sell, share or transfer your data to third parties.
  • It doesn't use your data for advertising, or to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • It has no analytics or tracking.

Notewell's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Sign out in Notewell → Settings → Sync to stop syncing. To remove Notewell's access and its sync file, go to myaccount.google.com/connections, choose Notewell, and remove its access. You can also delete the app's hidden data in Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Notewell → Delete hidden app data.
