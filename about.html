Notewell

A personal notes and flashcards app for Mac and iPhone.

Notewell keeps study notes and flashcards together: notes are organized like books by course and chapter, and cards made from a passage stay linked to it.

Google sign-in

Notewell can optionally sign in to Google to keep your own Mac and iPhone in sync. It uses only Google Drive's private app-data folder, a hidden area that only Notewell can see. It can't read or change any of your other files.
